Hands-on: NMS & CLI

How I work an access-network incident, from the first alarm to a closed ticket.

  1. DetectTicket or alarm on the dashboard and NMS
  2. ScopeOne customer, or a whole port or OLT?
  3. VerifyONU state, optical level and VLAN through CLI
  4. ActDispatch a technician, or escalate to ROC (L2) or DSO (L3)
  5. CloseConfirm with the customer, update the log, close the ticket
